**Figure 3. Review-level PICO-S decisions shape eligibility criteria.** **A**, Reviewers translate a review question into review-level population, intervention, comparator, outcome, and study-design definitions, which determine study eligibility. This formulation step is typically supplied by humans in current LLM-based systematic-review workflows. **B**, The lumper-splitter decision determines whether related interventions are pooled as a class or analyzed separately, illustrated here with newer-generation antidepressants. **C**, Eligibility rules for co-interventions should reflect clinical context: requiring an intervention to be delivered alone may exclude relevant evidence when it is normally paired with another treatment, whereas optional additions may modify the treatment effect. Blue and amber indicate alternative analytic or eligibility choices rather than correct and incorrect decisions.
